# Thesis writing instructions

- Edit the existing LaTeX thesis under `thesis/` and maintain its source register and abbreviations as writing progresses.
- The user's internship report is background material only. Do not cite it anywhere in the thesis or include it in `references.bib`.
- Describe the author's work directly. Preserve attribution to earlier prototypes, collaborators, and external work; do not imply inherited work is a new thesis contribution.
- Support technical claims with verified external sources. If a claim is supported only by project context, state it as project scope or identify the additional evidence needed rather than assigning an unrelated citation.
- Format subsection numbers compactly with no separator after the section number: for example, Section 1.4 subsections are 1.41, 1.42, and so on (not 1.4.1, 1.4.2). Apply the thesis-wide subsection format consistently.
